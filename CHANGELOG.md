## [0.1.0] - 2026-05-20

### Added

- Initial release of `MUD.Modules.ClampedContentSizeFitter`.
- `ClampedContentSizeFitter` component extending `ContentSizeFitter` with optional min/max width and height clamps.
- Custom inspector for enabling and editing clamp limits per fitted axis.
- Multi-page side-by-side comparison demo sample scene with Prev/Next switching and manual content controls.
- English and Chinese documentation, package metadata, and docs site scaffolding.
